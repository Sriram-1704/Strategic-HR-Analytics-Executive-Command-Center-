# 📊 Strategic HR Analytics Executive Command Center

## 📌 Project Overview

The Strategic HR Analytics Executive Command Center is an end-to-end Data Analytics project designed to analyze employee attrition, workforce demographics, employee satisfaction, compensation, and performance trends.

The project was built using Python, MySQL, Excel, and Power BI to transform raw HR data into meaningful business insights and an executive-level dashboard.

This project demonstrates the complete Data Analytics lifecycle:

- Data Understanding
- Data Cleaning
- Feature Engineering
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- SQL Analytics
- Business Insights Generation
- Dashboard Development
- Executive Reporting

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to identify:

- Why employees leave the organization
- Which departments are most affected
- Which job roles have higher attrition
- The impact of overtime on employee retention
- Employee demographics associated with attrition
- Workforce risk indicators for HR decision-making

## 🔍 Key Findings

### Attrition by Department

| Department | Attrition Rate |
|------------|----------------|
| Sales | 20.58% |
| Human Resources | 18.75% |
| Research & Development | 13.60% |

Sales department experienced the highest employee attrition.

---

### Attrition by Gender

| Gender | Attrition Rate |
|---------|---------------|
| Male | 16.85% |
| Female | 14.65% |

Male employees showed slightly higher attrition.

---

### Attrition by Marital Status

| Marital Status | Attrition Rate |
|----------------|---------------|
| Single | 25.32% |
| Married | 12.50% |
| Divorced | 9.94% |

Single employees demonstrated the highest turnover.

---

### Overtime Impact

| Overtime | Attrition Rate |
|-----------|---------------|
| Yes | 30.40% |
| No | 10.25% |

Employees working overtime were approximately 3 times more likely to leave.

---

### Top Job Roles by Attrition

| Job Role | Attrition Rate |
|-----------|---------------|
| Sales Representative | 40.48% |
| Laboratory Technician | 23.66% |
| Human Resources | 22.64% |
| Sales Executive | 17.27% |
| Research Scientist | 15.72% |

Sales Representatives had the highest attrition among all job roles.
